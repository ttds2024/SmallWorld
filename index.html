Chronicle

    Small Realm

    The island is too small for two peoples. Land your Humans on its shores, fight a rival band over 10 turns, and finish with the most victory coins. Every game is played on a freshly drawn island.

    • Conquer a region with 2 tokens, +1 per defender, +1 on a mountain.
    • Score 1 coin per region each turn, +1 for each farmland your Humans hold.
    • Decline when your band is spread thin: it stays on the map and keeps scoring, and a fresh band of 10 arrives.

    How to play

    1. Your band. You lead 10 Humans tokens. Each turn starts by picking up all your tokens but one from each region you hold.
    2. Landing. A band that holds no regions lands from the sea on any coastal region. After that, each conquest must be next to a region you already hold.
    3. Conquering costs 2 tokens, +1 for each token already there (a Lost Tribe counts as 1) and +1 on a mountain. Lakes can't be taken. Click a glowing region; its badge shows the cost.
    4. Battles. With Fight battles on, each attack is a siege: aim your catapult at the castle and bring its strength (40, +20 per token above 2 the attack costs) to zero before your shots run out (cost + 3). Fireballs (2 per siege) hit harder, splash and burn. A lost siege ends your conquests for the turn; you keep your tokens. Short of tokens, you storm with fewer shots instead of rolling the die.
    5. Final conquest. When you are short, you may make one last attack with at least one token: roll the reinforcement die (0, 0, 0, 1, 2, 3) and add it. Win or lose, that ends your conquests for the turn.
    6. Losses. A region's defender loses one token; the rest come back to them and are spread over their regions. Lost Tribes and Humans in decline are wiped out.
    7. Redeploy. When you stop, move tokens between your regions (each keeps at least one), then end the turn.
    8. Scoring. At the end of each turn: 1 coin per region you hold, in decline or not, +1 coin per farmland held by your active Humans.
    9. Decline. Instead of conquering, send your band into decline: one token stays on each region and keeps scoring (no farmland bonus), your old declined band leaves the map, and next turn a fresh band of 10 lands on the coast.
    10. Abandon. Before your first conquest of a turn you may pull all tokens out of a region back into your hand.
